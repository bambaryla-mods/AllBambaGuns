The gaps it fills

Vanilla RimWorld has conventional guns only at Industrial and charge guns only at Spacer. There are no plasteel-era bullets and no charge pistols, no Industrial charge prototypes, nothing past the charge rifle. This mod fills every empty cell below with a full line of guns, each with its own research, recipes and textures.

Seven guns per line

Every line has the same seven roles, so moving up a tier means swapping each gun for its better twin, not relearning the roster:

  • Pistol: a light sidearm.
  • Bullpup: a compact assault rifle that fires fast bursts. Not in vanilla.
  • Battle rifle: the standard rifle.
  • DMR: a designated marksman rifle. Lighter and quicker to aim than a sniper rifle, for a little less damage and range. Not in vanilla.
  • Shotgun: short range, heavy hits.
  • LMG: long bursts for suppression.
  • Sniper rifle: the longest reach in the line.

A line's first research unlocks its pistol and battle rifle. The bullpup and DMR need its specialized research, and the shotgun, LMG and sniper rifle its heavy research.

Disposable launchers

Single-use rocket launchers: a pawn fires it, then drops the empty tube. Each tier hits harder and reaches further, and the EMP versions disable electronics and mechanoids over a wide area. Designs inspired by Star Wars, Helldivers 2, Killzone and real launchers.

Under CE the launchers replace this mod's vanilla ones with CE's ballistics: armor-piercing APHE and HEAT warheads join HE, and the Solar EMP and both Kwazaari launchers fire several rockets before they're spent. They are crafted in batches, and the Industrial ones only need CE's simple launchers research.

Research

The mod's research lives in the All-Bamba Tech tab. With Beyond Our Reach, the Solar and Kwazaari projects join it, and Ultra moves after Solar: glitterworld ballistics also need BOR's solar weaponry, and the ultra and toxic launchers need solar launchers.

Under CE without Beyond Our Reach, the three glitterworld ballistic projects are removed (see above).

Compatibility

The mod needs nothing else to run. Everything else below is picked up on its own when present:

  • Combat Extended: every gun gets CE stats, calibers and ammo. The charge lines use the mod's own charged ammo with standard, concentrated and ion rounds, and charge rounds wear armor down twice as fast (the wearer takes normal damage).
  • Beyond Our Reach: adds the Solar and Kwazaari charge lines and launchers, and puts Ultra research after Solar.
  • Biotech: adds the ultra toxic launcher.
  • Muzzle Flash: every gun gets a muzzle flash.

Load it after Combat Extended and Beyond Our Reach. The mod declares this, so RimWorld's auto-sort handles it.
