Every line has the same seven roles, so moving up a tier means swapping each gun for its better twin, not relearning the roster. Use the switch at the top of the page to see the stats with or without Combat Extended. Hover over a gun's name for its in-game description. Under each name: the research it needs and the crafting skill to make it.

Seven guns per line

  • Pistol: a light sidearm.
  • Bullpup: a compact assault rifle that fires fast bursts. Not in vanilla.
  • Battle rifle: the standard rifle.
  • DMR: a designated marksman rifle. Lighter and quicker to aim than a sniper rifle, for a little less damage and range. Not in vanilla.
  • Shotgun: short range, heavy hits.
  • LMG: long bursts for suppression.
  • Sniper rifle: the longest reach in the line.

A line's first research unlocks its pistol and battle rifle. The bullpup and DMR need its specialized research, and the shotgun, LMG and sniper rifle its heavy research. The Research page lists every project.
