Single-use rocket launchers: a pawn fires it, then drops the empty tube. Each tier hits harder and reaches further, and the EMP versions disable electronics and mechanoids over a wide area. Designs inspired by Star Wars, Helldivers 2, Killzone and real launchers.

Under CE the launchers replace this mod's vanilla ones with CE's ballistics: armor-piercing APHE and HEAT warheads join HE, and the Solar EMP and both Kwazaari launchers fire several rockets before they're spent. They are crafted in batches, and the Industrial ones only need CE's simple launchers research.

Use the switch at the top of the page to see the stats with or without Combat Extended. A badge under a launcher's name marks the mod or DLC it needs.
