What the mod does

Vanilla RimWorld has conventional guns only at Industrial and charge guns only at Spacer. There are no plasteel-era bullets and no charge pistols, no Industrial charge prototypes, nothing past the charge rifle. This mod fills every empty cell below with a full line of guns, each with its own research, recipes and textures.

What you need

RimWorld {{ about.versions|join(" or ") }}. No other mod is required. Combat Extended, Beyond Our Reach, Biotech and Muzzle Flash each add something when they're loaded; Compatibility lists what.
